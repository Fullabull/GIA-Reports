The Mohammad Accords & Esoteric Non-Interference Lore

Core Concept: In esoteric, ufological, and "Inner Earth" ascension literature, the Mohammad Accords (also spelled Mohammed or Muhammad Accords) refer to a covert interdimensional treaty signed roughly 1,400 years ago during the era of Islamic expansion, regulating off-world operations on Earth.

1. The Core Purpose & The "Look Human" Rule

  • The Non-Interference Clause: Concluded between surface controllers, off-world factions, and various interdimensional entities (including reptilian and Orion groupings), the treaty was established to avoid violating cosmic non-interference directives regarding humanity's free-will evolution.
  • The Disguise Rule: Under the explicit terms of the Accords, any non-human or anti-human entity operating openly on the surface of the Earth is strictly required to present themselves in human form (whether through biological "avatar suits," physical shapeshifting, or energetic/holographic projection).
  • Behind-the-Scenes Manipulation: While the Accords restricted non-human beings from appearing openly in their natural physical states, they permitted covert control, telepathic influence, political infiltration, and manipulation from behind the scenes.

2. Why the Name & Spelling Is Suspicious

  • Historical Cover: Authors in these esoteric circles claim the treaty was named after the Prophet Muhammad because it was finalized during the 7th century AD on Earth.
  • Divergence from Physical-World Usage: The term causes confusion because, in standard geopolitical analysis, "Mohammad Accords" refers to a non-Western Middle Eastern security proposal drafted by analyst Hussein Askary to counter the U.S. Abraham Accords. In traditional Islamic history, it refers to historical treaties such as the Charter of Medina.

3. Key Researchers & Sources Who Discuss This Concept

The primary sources for this treaty—along with broader frameworks regarding off-world treaties, human disguise rules, and planetary quarantine—come from a distinct group of ascension researchers, contactees, and channeled materials:

  1. Wes Penre (The Wes Penre Papers / Synthetic Matrix)
    Focus: Details the Mohammed Accords in his Papers on the Multiverse. Penre focuses on cosmic treaties, the Orion/Sirian influence on Earth's matrix, and how non-human entities must follow specific galactic rules to maintain human incarnation and covert planetary control.
  2. Cobra (The Portal / 2012 Portal)
    Focus: An influential figure in the ascension movement who explicitly references the Mohammad Accords in monthly updates, discussing them as an ancient treaty limiting open non-human intervention—noting efforts by "Inner Earth" factions and surface forces to renegotiate or revoke them.
  3. George Kavassilas
    Focus: Ex-ascension insider who talks extensively about off-world agreements, cosmic legal frameworks, and the mechanisms non-human beings use to inhabit human "costumes" or avatar bodies while operating on Earth.
  4. Barbara Marciniak (Channeled Material / Bringers of the Dawn)
    Focus: While not using the specific title "Mohammad Accords," her channeled Pleiadian material laid the foundation for this lore by describing the "Law of Non-Interference" and how off-world groups had to insert themselves into human lineages rather than appearing in their natural forms.
  5. Michael Salla (Exopolitics)
    Focus: Examines "Galactic Agreements," secret space program pacts, and diplomatic treaties made between non-human factions and Earth leaders governing non-interference and human representation.
  6. Corey Goode & Alex Collier
    Focus: Contactee and whistleblower accounts covering galactic treaties, "Law of One" style non-intervention pacts, and the strict protocols non-human groups must follow when walking among surface humans.
