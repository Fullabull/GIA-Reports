Summary

In this three-hour special of the Truth, Honor and Integrity show, Thomas Williams is joined by Kim, M, A, and the Universal Council to discuss humanity’s origins, spiritual evolution, secret histories, and the effort to liberate Earth from long-standing control systems. They touch on extraterrestrial influences, the Draco, Atlantis and Lemuria, the concept of ascension, the role of religion, and suppressed technologies. The tone is deeply spiritual, urgent, and collaborative, focused on healing, rebuilding, and embracing Source connection.
