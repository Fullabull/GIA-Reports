Summary

Kim opens the September 5, 2026 report by framing Part Four as background -- a brief "how and why" meant to set up Part Five's first real status update, and ideally one of the last installments before Beyond the Matrix has no Matrix left to report on.

She traces the Matrix to an ancient War: humans -- among them the Abraxas, along with the Draco and other species -- conquered galaxies and enslaved rivals, including Homo sapiens on Earth, who became prisoners of war. The conquerors became the Royals: self-modified humans and allied species who took the ruling class positions after the War but stayed off-world themselves, ruling Earth instead through Bloodline Families installed here in their place. Twelve rulers across four kingdoms share four goals, Kim discloses: supreme rulership over the Multiverse; favoring science over Source and the Divine Plan, since Source's Secret Sauce cannot be defined or controlled; rewriting the Divine Plan to replace that Secret Sauce with Ethereum-based energy, which fails without it; and conquering Source and all of Creation outright, treating every being as a resource to power their machines.

Kim reports Earth's standing as the Guardian of Planets, or nexus planet: every planet links directly to Source, but Earth alone receives and relays the Divine Plan to keep the others in Harmony, which is what made her the primary target once the kingdoms grasped the leverage. In a natural world, she adds, that harmony extends to daily life: planets adjust automatically for population and resources, there is no manufactured scarcity or climate change, and each person can co-create a self-sustaining "micro-environment" home -- no artificial heating, no outside energy grid, no cut trees or mined ore -- private from passersby while still open to the nature around it. The same fight for control extended to Suns -- each being's individual Sol, and Earth's own Central Sun, originally seated at Alcyone -- which the kingdoms conquered and mirrored into artificial "black suns," including a Black Sun installed over Earth.

Kim splits the conflict into two sides: her own, the Melchizedeks (Source's warriors, who want no rulership of their own), against the twelve rulers -- divided into "six and six" and known on Earth as the Deep State, Merovingians, Satanists, and Luciferians. She discloses that simulations are built on a Three-Times-Three protocol, a Star-of-David-style geometry binding a ruler's soul to the machine at its center, and that the ruling leaders literally feed their own consciousness into the Machine from stasis to run and override it -- four such structures currently active, differing only in how harshly each treats its subjects. Kim adds that she has repeatedly placed herself as the gate between the Kingdom of Source and the Simulation, at a cost she states bluntly: stabbed in the head four times and twice in the heart over the course of this work.

She closes by reframing the Deep State as management staff rather than ultimate authority -- foremen overridable by the kingdoms above them, their corporations answering to the Empire rather than acting independently -- and promises Part Five, the first genuine "here's where we're at" update, later the same day.
