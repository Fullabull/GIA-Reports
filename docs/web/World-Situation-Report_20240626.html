Summary

Kim opens the June 26, 2024 report by completing the round of neurological disturbance sites tied to LED lights and electronics, from Sweden and Okinawa to Mount Shasta. The sites were predominantly alien tech running on standing orders, and the Deep State cannot rebuild them at that scale.

On banking, operatives keep the banks chasing claims of unleveraged gold and pushing digital cash held outside the system. Meanwhile, fixers working for the Families -- the genetic shareholders of banks -- arrive in Durango from JPMorgan Chase and Bank of New York Mellon, following preliminary talks with JPMorgan's potential interim chairman. Kim advises that banks survive only as service providers, not custodians, with a seat on the board as the price of cooperation.

After the Deep State's sundown expectation came to nothing, Megvii tried to install its Great Reset social crediting platform everywhere. It failed without the computing power of Omega, Starlight (a Lucifer program), or Omega Project Stargate. Kim then discloses the Farms: bred, medically enhanced Black Ops operatives conditioned for loyalty to each U.S. president since the 22nd except Biden. Their farm accounts hold liens and Money Four (M4) derivatives from presidential spoils. A Monday attempt to seize the Farm Monetary System ended with every farm system and Langley's backdoors gone.

Kim closes on July 4, the next expected payday. The major annual Alpha system allocations, routed through the Fed to HSBC Hong Kong for trading, last went out on July 4, 2012, sent by Kim herself. A large 2018 allocation was pulled back, and a small U.S.-only one followed in 2019, but none are coming now. Every asset anywhere has already been leveraged, and Kim is content to keep throwing out carrots for the Deep State to chase if it means they leave her side alone.
