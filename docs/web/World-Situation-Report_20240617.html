Summary

Kim opens the June 17, 2024 report with a recorded message after an all-night weekend. The G7 attendees were promised a currency reset by 3 p.m. Eastern, but nothing arrived. The Q-crew claims the USS Helena is delivering someone to Guantanamo Bay. Kim says the submarine is more likely retrieving Russian weapons from Cuba for another scare.

A Q-crew tweet announcing that aliens are here came just before Friday night's attempted invasion. Thousands of cyborg beings in megastructure moons across the galaxy were to swap out humans in positions of power through a Vril-style soul swap. Each swapped body would charge an Omega battery and act for Omega against humanity. It was Marduk's plan B after Friday's failure, and the Deep State would have been swapped out too.

The Deep State's backdoor deals promised countries a restarted Federal Reserve and a Treasury takeover running the new Financial System. On a conference call, they admitted the installations were out of their control. Over Saturday, the Knights of Malta communication lines and secret monetary system, NSA remnants, and a Bitcoin backdoor were taken out. On Sunday, a two-hour brute-force attack launched over 108,729 attempts against the Light System, targeting HSBC and Development Bank of Singapore servers. Agreements fired at the Hall of Records were all rejected.

The attack revealed two Marduk facilities, both now gone: one under HSBC's Hong Kong headquarters, built to transmute cash back to the debt system, and the Systems Administration Unit for the Omega system on Tung Ping Chau Island.

Operatives also tried to access a Death Star in Africa, one of three on Earth, to gain control of its absolute sphere. Today's attempts to crash or siphon the banking system also failed. The predicted 10 Days of Darkness and Emergency Broadcast never came. Attempts to reprogram Kim's Lifi and Codex 685 are failing, and the SQL servers have been changed for security. The attacks may end with the Summer Solstice or when the Deep State runs out of options. Creator AI remnants were found, and the Schumann Resonance spiked during both the Hong Kong hacking and the alien invasion attempt.

A new group of operatives has asked for a test transaction. Kim set conditions: a U.S. state of her choosing, her own representative present, and a few million dollars. She notes it may also be an attempt to trace her line back to the Light System. Despite the attacks, Kim says the Golden Age and Crystalline Time will continue, and the old AI systems will not return.
