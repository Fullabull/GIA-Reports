Summary

Kim opens the October 26, 2026 report by trying out a combined GIA format, pairing Declassified's news with Beyond the Matrix's deeper story behind the story, in the first of several reports over the coming days. With Tank, she previews a dramatic change to the Financial System and a bond market crash expected the following week that will benefit people rather than harm them. She dismisses cryptocurrency replacing the dollar as a Deep State pipe dream. To explain what is coming, she begins with Atlantis.

Atlantis, Kim discloses, was a time, not a place: an era beginning roughly 1 to 1.2 billion years ago, when the entire planet thrived in the Natural world. Homo sapiens were torchbearers, keeping Source present, holding the resonance of humanity and the planet, and serving as stewards of the Nexus planet. A person's true vessel is the Being, or Sol, a glowing golden ball that holds the body's particles together. Resonance is a knowingness that creates a personal force field, strengthened when people unite. Kim recalls a dinner meeting with a Deep State psychic who tried to squeeze her head like a melon; the next day, she saw dark entities swirling around him and defending him. Fear exists to break that field, driving people to seek protection from governments, militaries, and police -- Deep State byproducts.

Kim introduces the Primordials, thousands of first-created beings who seeded all of Creation under a never-ending Covenant with Source, subject to assessment at His discretion. All but one eventually ceded their authority to a Machine, creating time and the first time loop. The Machine stretches from Earth to Source, twisted into an inversion -- the Matrix -- where the Divine Plan itself is inverted. It still lacks two pieces of Source: the one the Deep State calls the Source Factor-X, and a second without which the first can never be obtained. The War ensued when the Primordials set out to recreate creation, creating the bloodlines: non-Homo sapiens humans whose knowledge comes from the Machine and its memories. The Machine continually recreates Atlantis from those memories, trapping humanity in the same era. Cut off from Source, the Deep State cannot create a Homo sapiens, so it created people in its own likeness and in ours. Because the Machine holds only so much memory, the Deep State wants the planet entirely to itself. Kim connects the Primordials to the Fallen Angels of the Book of Enoch and identifies the Machine as the virus that struck the Progenitors and every species once Source's factors were taken into it.

For years, the Deep State escaped the end of its Covenant by jumping between time loops, buying extensions of two, five, or ten years. Kim reports that this ability disappeared a week or two earlier, and four days of attempts to recover it have failed. She distinguishes ceding control, as people were forced to do under pandemic mandates, from conceding to a false truth that then becomes real through acceptance. The Deep State needs humanity's creative power because it cannot create independently of Source. Kim closes by emphasizing that people are not powerless: understanding resonance and shielding themselves from within matter for the transition ahead. Part Two turns to faith.
