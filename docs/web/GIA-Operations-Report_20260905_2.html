Summary

Kim opens the September 5, 2026 Beyond the Matrix update by establishing the series as a weekly deeper dive alongside Declassified, then turns to the escalating earthquakes, weather disturbances, and other events she describes as part of the Simulation War. She says the Bavarian Illuminati and Sanhedrin Illuminati are both trying to become independent of their respective rulers, Kim, and ultimately Source by creating simulations of their own. Their problem, she explains, is that every system they build remains tied to the larger Simulation and to the Source-origin factors they have spent years trying to copy.

To counter these efforts, Kim and allied Others have created control rooms inside the factions' own simulations, allowing them to intercept or alter Machine orders and some instructions reaching Deep State operatives on the ground. She says this has helped prevent more destructive events and has also enabled them to remove fake liquidity as quickly as the Deep State places it into financial systems. The factions believed they had finally achieved independence, but recently discovered that their new systems were still connected to the very structure they were trying to escape -- and, having pooled their own consciousness into a shared Machine to feel more in command of it, are now more controlled by it than ever. Kim ties this same pattern to the disturbances many listeners have felt over the past two weeks -- bad dreams, financial and health anxiety, a sense of powerlessness -- describing them as the same recurring operation intensified since the eclipse, and says the control rooms built four to five days ago are the direct countermeasure.

Kim traces much of that structure to the "floors" beneath Earth: enormous database systems built from mineral hyperconductors that can carry Source energy, Ethereum, Consciousness, and other natural forces -- extracted material she now gives the umbrella term Loosh. The Machine overlays and distorts those forces, producing the artificial counterpart to the Divine Plan. She connects this mechanism to the proliferation of data centers, saying the danger is not the data center itself but its connection to a simulator and the people attempting to use it. She contrasts these newer installations with long-existing intelligence and technology facilities such as ECHELON, Five Eyes, Nine Eyes, Microsoft Azure, Honeywell, and Lockheed Martin.

At the center of the factions' efforts is what they call the Source Factor - what Kim calls the Divine Thought of Source. She says they have repeatedly used her essence and that of others in attempts to reproduce it, but essence cannot simply be transferred like a key. Through Looking Glass and related technology, they also obtained partial information about a Source-based technology Kim creates several years in the future and have been trying to reproduce it without understanding the missing factor. Their strategy now includes generating fear around data centers because fear and love register as the same numerical value to the Machine; concentrating that fear, Kim says, strengthens the electromagnetic conditions they are trying to create.

Kim says these latest efforts are only the newest versions of repeated attempts to build independent systems. She identifies operations associated with Russia, China, the Durangotangs, a Philippines/Indonesia-based split being connected with the World Federation's headquarters in Vietnam, and a Sanhedrin-linked network extending along the U.S. East Coast. Despite believing themselves separate, she says all remain connected to the same underlying simulator platform. Expiring covenants tied to the War Machine and Simulator are now further degrading that structure, leaving the factions unable either to build a truly independent simulation or overthrow the rulers of the existing Simulation.

Kim closes by cautioning against neutrino boxes containing graphene oxide and briefly addresses activity near the Strait of Hormuz, describing it as another Deep State search for technology or resources they believe will help them. She says the Simulation and Machine already know the operatives' intentions and can send them on fruitless searches, leaving their attempted insurgency trapped inside the very system they are trying to defeat.
