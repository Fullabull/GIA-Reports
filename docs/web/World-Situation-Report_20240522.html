Summary

Kim opens the May 22, 2024 report with the Deep State waiting. They hope tomorrow's Full Moon will help their Marduk program before Friday's deadline, when operatives expect $56 billion, after which they plan to crash the United States, burn it to the ground, and revive it under their control. Their rumors of cashable 1924 German bonds and $300 trillion on ledger are lies. A call between Tom Melville and Trump's lawyers keeps being pushed off; there is chatter that the operatives will ditch Trump once they get the money. Kim has asked Tom to tell them she has acquired DARPA's Global Voting System base platform and will be voting for Jill Stein -- her side can now put anyone on the ballot, in almost any country.

While clearing Marduk's time capsules, set to trigger for millennia, Kim uncovered new information about Earth's role in Creation. Earth is the Keystone for all Creation and a Celestial itself, and its All-That-Is Keystone had Dark, Neutral, and Light sides, so what Earth's inhabitants create affects the entire Multiverse. That is why so many beings are arriving: to hold the Light. Earth was first inhabited by Junior Celestials. The Merovingians (Dark) and the Melchizedeks (Light) often number about 15,000 each, and both sides compete for the Neutral Celestials; intermarriage produces Neutrals such as Prince William. The rest of humanity are organic life forms with less creative power who can be swayed either way, which is why the other side is so fixated on bloodlines. Matter first formed only once there was Light and Dark, but Source has now made a way to create matter from the Light side alone.

Marduk is a title, the Lord of the Seas: an enforcer of balance among Light, Dark, and Neutral Creation. The being who held it moved from avatar to avatar around Earth for some 75 million years. He used the Dial of Destiny to claim humans chose Darkness, genetically manipulated humans, plants, animals, and Earth toward it, and ran his own Growth Rate of Development (GOD) program, which required 92% of humanity to be awake before people could receive energy directly from Source -- then switched it off when humanity reached 76%, around 1982. Society suppressed Light-side Celestials from childhood while the Dark side identified and nurtured its own, denying humanity the genuine choice Marduk claimed it had. Only Celestials could have stopped him, and no one did until now; about 2,500 on the Light side are waking up.

Within the last 12 hours, Earth stopped being a Keystone of Light, Dark, and Neutral Creation and now creates an All-That-Is of the Golden Age, Crystalline Time, and Source. The Dark side will no longer be able to manifest anything Dark, and the programs that helped it are going away; whether Dark Celestials simply lose their power or physically leave is unknown. Kim's team has just acquired a genomic history of Celestials and hopes to post numbers.

Transfers remain hung up near the end by customer portals, the extra GOD program, and Marduk's leftovers. Once humans receive Source energy with no haircut, their power to manifest will grow, and the other side's Industrial Psychics can no longer counter them. Kim asks those who do cleanup and grid work to revisit past sites, and everyone to manifest positively and ground to what is now a positive Keystone.
