Summary

Kim opens the October 2, 2026 report, joined by Tank, with Senator John Kennedy calling Iran's leaders reptiles and President Xi the "chief reptile" among China, North Korea, Russia, and Iran -- part of renewed alien-invasion hype that Kim calls staging, not disclosure. Tank outlines End Times prophecies involving the yellow jinn, the Mahdi, al-Masih ad-Dajjal, and Gog and Magog. Kim ties them to Yom Kippur, when Merovingian bloodlines, including the Kennedy family, went to sites worldwide with artifacts, hoping the Machine would choose them to help stage Armageddon and then present themselves as humanity's saviors.

Kim discloses Palang in Yunnan, officially uninhabited, as a breeding ground where female infants spared from infanticide are taken and later used to perpetuate Asian royal and Deep State bloodlines. Other Colors have their own breeding locations. Tank recalls missing children, CPS, the Clintons, and Senator Nancy Schaefer's investigation. Kim then describes an alleged FlyDubai hijacking -- an Indian copilot traveling on an Omani passport supposedly stabbing the pilot -- as a false flag intended to blame Iran, provoke Israeli retaliation, and advance another world-war scenario.

Behind the larger operation are three women -- one in China, one beneath the United Nations, and one in Japan -- to whom Kim says the world's Deep State is now bowing. They sent Xi to meet Trump to discuss pandemic payouts, costs, and how many Americans could be lost without sacrificing control of the Machine. Their plan was a Marburg-like pandemic using a boomslang-venom substance manufactured at 20 sites in China, intended to begin October 2 and no later than October 5. The lead Chinese woman also tried to open the "floodgates" controlling water in human bodies and across the planet, but could not bring anything through the gate and came after Kim instead. Kim says the woman is now gone and the Simulation cannot bring in money or its own creations unless the gate's controller allows it.

Kim recalls that in 2018 an operative warned her that if "they" were not back in control by the 2019 UN General Assembly, they would release a global pandemic; Tank confirms Kim told him this before COVID. Kim says the last 10 days have been one of the hardest battles she has fought in years. When Tank asks whether the current operation depended on getting her to release money, Kim clarifies that they were repeatedly trying to get her to open the gate -- including the Chinese woman herself. More follows in Beyond the Matrix.
