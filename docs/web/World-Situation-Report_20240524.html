Summary

Kim opens the May 24, 2024 report with the Full Moon, which passed without the event the operatives expected. Recent earthquakes in Nevada, California, and Texas come from attempts to power the Omega system with nuclear power at the Nevada Test Site and Axiom, a classified Monarch Military program at Texas A&M, after Kim's side cut Omega's power. It is not the first try. For years the Yakuza shipped Fukushima uranium rods to Antarctica under a deal with Bush Senior and Kissinger; when the rods went unpaid, the Yakuza killed Bush Senior in 2014. An attempt to restore worldwide banking to its 1999 configuration, to run it on the nuclear-powered Omega system, also failed, leaving the promised payouts no closer. Kim dismisses rumors of a Memorial Day weekend banking shutdown, assuring viewers that cards and ATMs should remain available.

In Syria, Pallavicini family turf, the First Lady and President were flown to London and Moscow for invented illnesses, tied to deals to give up power for a settlement. Iranian intelligence has seen the alleged footage of their president and is not buying it; a leak puts him in Belize, where Trump's body (he died of cancer about two years ago) was taken for a Lower Astral ceremony. The EPA is an NGO under Global Headquarters and the Order of the Black Sun, and the centralized water-release systems tied to it, formerly run by Omega, are now under Kim's side's control worldwide, ending an SSP plan to infect the U.S. population.

Kim closes with news that her team's essential oils are coming soon, with details on the countries and farms they are sourced from.
