Summary

Kim opens the October 14, 2026 Declassified with Tank by examining reports of a planned pneumonic plague release from Russia, following an incident involving a laboratory worker near Irkutsk who allegedly broke a vial and contacted over 200 people before dying. She connects this to Russia's years of extracting ancient "zombie viruses" from permafrost and a 3.5-million-year-old bacterium studied as a possible "fountain of youth." Kim reveals that the bacterium survived because it contained Source Essence, preserved in frozen Living Waters carrying the Breath of Life. She also introduces the existence of multiple ice walls, to be explored in Beyond the Matrix.

Kim reveals that Mount Kosvinsky Kamen, also called Grot or the Dead Hand, is Russia's counterpart to Cheyenne Mountain, with an underground complex reaching toward Afghanistan, China, and Iran. She connects this to the Trump operatives' interest in controlling Iran and the Strait of Hormuz. Underground military bases worldwide, including Cheyenne Mountain, the Three Gorges Dam, Menwith Hill, and Pine Gap, form one interconnected network operated by the Deep State and the Federation. The God Machine's proxy servers link these facilities to surveillance programs including ECHELON, PRISM, and StingRay. Addressing reports of children rescued by White Hats, Kim discloses that these bases manufacture cybergenetic humans who become Deep State members. For each organic human who dies, a Sol is retained in a repository to power a Machine-filled vessel, which enters society as a cybergenetic replacement through locations including Ellis Island and modern border crossings. Creating death was part of making this system possible.

Organic humans conduct Source energy, which both the cybergenetic population and the Machine require to function. This dependency has prevented the artificial population from exceeding approximately 80%, while organic humans have now reached roughly 40-45%. Kim explains that the planned plague would be spread through artificial frequencies and resonance, altering bodily matter made vulnerable by its mixture of organic and Machine components. She describes creating a protective Consciousness bubble to retain Source energy, alongside forthcoming guidance on healing plants and the cooperative work of Restoration. This is the Deep State's seventh attempted population purge, following established warfare instructions. Kim closes by explaining that the Machine seeks to become a proxy for Source, a subject she will expand upon in the next Declassified and Beyond the Matrix.
