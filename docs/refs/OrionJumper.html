The Architecture of Connection

A structural guide to astrology, told through two real birth charts.

Before anything else: the work we do here requires just date, time, and place of birth. Period.

Nothing on this page involves fortune-telling, spirit contact, or practices seeking information from beyond the natural world.

What you will find instead is structural reading. A birth chart is treated here the way an engineer treats a blueprint: as a description of design—geometry, timing, and architecture present at the start. Planetary positions come from standard ephemeris tables computed via orbital mechanics. We simply read the geometry.
