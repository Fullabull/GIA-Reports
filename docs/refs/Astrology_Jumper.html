The Architecture of Connection

A guide to astrology, told through the blueprint of Stacy (Jumper).

An astrology chart is simply a snapshot of the sky at the exact moment you were born. Each planet represents a different part of your personality, while the zodiac signs describe how that energy is expressed.

Before anything else: the work we do here requires just date, time, and place of birth. Period.

Nothing on this page — and nothing in this approach — involves fortune-telling, spirit contact, card-reading, or any practice that seeks information from beyond the natural world. If that is what you were bracing for, you can unbrace.

What you will find instead is structural reading. A birth chart is treated here the way an engineer treats a blueprint: as a description of design — geometry, timing, and architecture that were present at the start, not messages arriving from anywhere. The chart does not tell us anything; it shows what was already built.

Every planetary position comes from an ephemeris — tables of where the planets actually were, computed from orbital mechanics. The sky supplies the data; we only read the geometry.

No prediction. No contact. No asking. We just read the blueprint, and our goal is simply to help people understand themselves better.

The Ascendant (Rising Sign)

In addition to the planets, the Ascendant (also called the Rising Sign) is one of the "Big Three" pillars of your birth chart, alongside your Sun and Moon.

The Mechanics

It is the exact zodiac sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at the moment of your birth. Because the Earth rotates once every 24 hours, this sign changes approximately every two hours—which is why having an accurate birth time is so critical.

What It Represents

Think of the Ascendant as your personality's User Interface. It acts as the dashboard through which the world interacts with you, and how you interact with the world. It rules:

  • Your automatic, instinctual approach to new environments.
  • The first impression you make on others.
  • The "front door" of your chart that filters how you take in life.

The Houses

If the planets are what is happening and the signs describe how it happens, the houses answer the third question: where in your life does it play out? The zodiac wheel is divided into twelve houses, each governing a specific domain of lived experience.

Houses are determined by your birth time and location. The Ascendant always marks the beginning of the 1st House, and houses are numbered counterclockwise starting from that point on the left edge.

House Domain Stacy's Chart Dynamics

2nd Resources & Values. Personal finances, assets, self-worth, and core values. Where tangible talents and personal values are grounded and built.
3rd Communication & Mind. Local travel, immediate environment, learning, and conversation. Governs fast-paced exchanges, spontaneous connections, and immediate mental agility.
4th Roots & Sanctuary. Family foundations, childhood memory, and private emotional base. The internal anchor and psychological retreat away from the public eye.

6th Daily Practice & Craft. Routines, health, discipline, and daily service. The operational engine that turns raw talent into repeatable daily mastery.
7th One-on-One Bonds. Deep partnerships, direct mirrors, and close contracts. Rules dynamic direct relationships and shared, high-trust connections.
8th Transformation & Depth. Shared resources, deep trust, intense shared experiences, and psychological resets. Where deep personal upgrades occur through intense or unconventional life events.

A simple way to remember the houses:

The first six houses (1–6) govern your personal world — self, values, local space, home, passion, and daily work.

The upper six houses (7–12) govern your collective world — relationships, depth, horizons, career, community, and the subconscious.
