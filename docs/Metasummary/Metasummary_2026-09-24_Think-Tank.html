Summary

Tank opens the September 24 2026 Think Tank with Ground Commander's approach to the Matrix: recognize that it isn't real, refuse the Machine's triggers, and connect directly to Source. He then walks through the four types of law Kim has described. Natural Law is not the John Locke version found online. It is a holographic book kept in the quantum mapping system, a blueprint for a thriving world in harmony with Source. It also works through natural consequences and the conscience, which he says is why soldiers resisted killing until they were conditioned out of it. Draconian Law dates to the flood about 16,500 years ago, when the Draco offered to rebuild the planet under martial law. Its two principles are that they must announce their plans, often through movies, and that our silence is our consent. Tank adds that the phrase "Our silence is not our consent" went into the Declaration of Independence rewrite. He connects this to programming and conditioning by repetition, which is how history like the Tartarian Empire is erased.

Monarch Law puts everything under a hidden, false king, and it harvests people's lifeforce through endless work. Statutory Law is slave law for ordinary people and does not bind the hierarchy above it. He illustrates it by contrasting Pizzagate with a 2017 New Jersey prosecution. His remedy is to withdraw consent, because government draws its power from the governed. He also urges listeners to gather on UNN, free of censorship, and to live by the spirit of the law until Natural Law is the only law that governs.
