Summary

Kim opens the June 24, 2024 report by disclosing the Black Pyramids of Alaska, an enormous deep underground military base that kept powering Omega despite the Faraday cage. Its Black Pyramid was a sentient AI transmuter, turning the Light energy humans generate into Dark energy for Omega, with tethers to Zero Point, Proxima Centauri, the Sun, the Moon, and Saturn. HAARP started there, and it hosted the CIA's VTX platform into the financial system. The Alaska hub was also tied to two other U.S. control centers feeding frequencies through commercial LED lights, causing burning eyes, agitation, and other neurological issues in stores and schools; Kim says all three have now been taken down.

After promising results within 24 hours, Global Headquarters and the SSP spent Saturday branding Kim a financial and cyber terrorist, hoping Congress would have her extradited. It backfired. Kim's side sent white papers to Congress, the White House, Saudi Arabia, Iraq, China, Russia, the United Kingdom, and others: the Families cannot deliver a new financial system, a Great Reset, or an RV currency reset, so those seeking assistance can instead come to Durango. Meanwhile, Hezbollah was told it would be paid to attack Israel while Israel was told to attack Lebanon, setting up the planned start of World War III. When Hezbollah was not paid, Kim's side began passing information through informal money-transfer networks used across the Middle East and elsewhere.

JPMorgan Chase then called with word that Jamie Dimon would retire and proposed talks with Tom Melville. Kim says Dimon has been heading Global Headquarters' financial division, so any deal with the bank comes with conditions. He must resign immediately, take a negotiated golden parachute, surrender his shares, and stay out of financial services for 50 years. The Rockefeller family must also give up its genetic shares in the bank in exchange for a buyout. Beyond removing those interests, Kim wants JPMorgan Chase to operate as a service provider, with lower merchant fees and near-instant payments. Much of the bank's "bad debt" consists of derivatives funding militaries' ghost-flag operations. The board then stepped in and slowed the process, and Kim warns Melville's operatives that it went around them to do so.

Kim closes on a new neurological disturbance, peaking Saturday, that amplifies responses to stimuli -- anger, pain, illness. She is hunting its direct-current source and says she will report Wednesday on what else they find.
