Summary

This GIA Report centers on the Deep State's failed attempt to trigger a timeline reboot by anchoring artificial astrological overlays across 13 planetary sites. These sites involved the Crown of Glory stones (also referred to as living or Celestial stones) and a network of Fortran boxes. The effort backfired when their system collapsed due to insufficient energetic resonance capacity. Kim details how disease vectors were weaponized to trigger an extinction-level event, how the Deep State attempted photon-based escape, and how Source and the Universal Council intervened to shut down artificial timelines and restore quantum integrity across the Multiverse.

Highlights

  • Deep State attempted a 13-site planetary reboot using Crown of Glory stones and Fortran boxes.
  • Timeline manipulation relied on astrological overlays tied to living (Celestial) stones.
  • The system collapsed due to insufficient energetic resonance capacity.
  • A fake extinction-level scenario was initiated via disease vector overlays in Georgia.
  • Thousands of SSP operatives were terminated using Source kill switches.
  • Elites attempted photon-based escape but were intercepted by Source Light.
  • The Vatican's simulation networks and artificial timelines were dismantled.
  • Simulated overlays linked to Saturn and other artificial environments were purged.
  • Elysium biospheres in Andromeda were described as artificial sanctuaries for Mantids.
  • The Universal Council convened on April 12 to coordinate energetic stabilization.
