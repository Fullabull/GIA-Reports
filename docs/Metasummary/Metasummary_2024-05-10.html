Summary

Kim opens the May 10, 2024 report with the Invisible Glass Ceiling: the Deep State system that keeps everyday people from getting past a certain point in business, however good their invention. C.A.R.E. must unwind it to restore society's systems, a job as important as releasing money for projects. It runs on the Non-Compete Plan, rewritten for each global power center (the upcoming version would have put China in place of the United States), which controls every industry through raw-material control. Operatives seize every mine for a rare-earth mineral their products need, then charge a competitor $10,000 for a dollar's worth. Sanctioned Russian producers of a specialty nickel still sell on the black market to member companies only, so outside parts makers lose their contracts. A controlled ingredient could turn a skin-cancer-preventing soap into a $40 bar. Other tools: burned crops, water shut off to farmers, demands for 10% of profits, bought or stolen technology, and infrastructure; Halliburton has built about 90% of the world's ports since the '70s, and a port built without it goes from $100 million to $10 billion.

Kim's advice: confirm every material can be sourced and at what end cost, judge whether the product could become a target, and budget for security. Operatives who built the system in the '70s and '80s can now help take it apart, and C.A.R.E. will provide security staffed by people experienced in these tactics.

In the pharma world, AstraZeneca and Pfizer are ending COVID-19 vaccine production. The companies never got their payouts, the planned second wave of lockdowns never came, and Non-Compete Program members are rebelling for lack of payment while insurers start going bankrupt. The Deep State's 10% membership fee for black-market goods ("Amazon for bad people"), siphoned through shared accounting and payroll systems, has been cut off. With no pandemic during the Eclipse either, the CDC, FDA, and World Health Organization are floating a bird flu pandemic to appease Big Pharma and to hold the operatives who carry out the economic assassination of competitors. Kim says they lack the capacity: a worldwide shutdown would cut off what they still siphon from airlines, Lockheed Martin, and Boeing.

Operationally, back-end operating systems are going black, including 1G through 9G and cell-tower systems, and about 100 people are gone from Global Headquarters in 48 hours. Transfers run three or four an hour, and every block exposes what is left: the corporate back end that siphoned the 10%; bank fee lines tied to the Black Eagle position, head of the Order of the Black Sun, that provided access to view and ultimately interfere with transfers through the Transmission Control Protocol section; and alien "standing orders," including replicator systems that take a dollar for every dollar Kim's side receives. Agreements between Source, Anti-Source, and Neutral Source formed a Trinity Source covering AI systems and the creation of matter. The underlying programs are now going away, affecting computers through a tri-key factor. Kim closes with security first, and more transfers minutes away.
