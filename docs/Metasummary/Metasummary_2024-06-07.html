Summary

Kim opens the June 7, 2024 report with the fallout from the previous day's New Moon: the Deep State's plans failed, and a rebel group of militaries worldwide has begun what look like three days of meetings to compare notes and decide a direction. The Trump operatives are not invited; their contract sources fell apart amid angry calls from Gulf Cooperation Council partners and missing money, though they still expect results within three days of the New Moon. Their Go It Alone Program has tried screen scraping the Global Repository, installing new currencies, and using old system keys and Marduk's leftover Trojan horses, none of which produces allocation numbers. Trump Organization lawyers called demanding Kim cease and desist, then backed down when she told them to serve her legally so she could present her evidence in court, including government fund transfers to Kim Jong Un.

Kim recounts a call from an Order of the Black Sun contact she had not spoken to in ten years. He proposed she give them everything and get nothing, while helicopters circled her house. When he claimed their losses could be counted on one hand, she listed sites around the world where their people had been found, stressing Atlanta, Croatia, and Berlin, and their people went straight to those locations within 24 hours. He also said elites like Warren Buffett and Zuckerberg are selling stock ahead of a plan to shut down the Financial System on August 1. Kim compares it to the 2013 plan she heard from Sheldon Adelson to crash the U.S. Financial System and move power to China; nobody moved then, and she expects a transition, not a crash, now. She explains that the sell-off is intended to fund DARPA, SSP, and Deep State operations while they wait for Lions Gate in August. Their plans came from Marduk, who operated in the gray between Alpha and Omega, and without him the playbook can never be finished. Kim operated solely in Alpha before creating the Light System.

She considers how governments might continue: rank-and-file militaries led by new "parents" from her side, shielded by the GIA's clearance, seem more workable than corralling politicians. The Order also tried to revive the Nazi Hydra Program, which sat above Project Paperclip and pursued genetic modification and hybrid humans with the SSP and the ACIO. Attempts involving Li-Fi, 5G, frequencies, and cancer biowarfare failed, many of them tied to Hydra. The Order believes its bloodline entitles it to rule and has promised Trump operatives the North American sector, but its alien technology and backing are gone. Kim traces the real source of past plans to Marduk, from Gorbachev's three hundred trillion dollars to Lenin's 100-year plan, and notes that Antarctica's resources, like all of Earth's, were already leveraged. Earth's gold belongs to humanity for co-creation under the broken 250,000-year-old deal.

She invites listeners to explore their former physical vessels, points to Walter Russell's work on Love and the interconnectedness of Creation, and explains why the transition cannot be rushed without risking catastrophe. Everyone has a part to play: keep anchoring Source Light, nurture positive outcomes, and find joy in everyday life.
