Summary

Kim opens the May 15, 2024 report by explaining that the "Year of the Cicada" in alt and mainstream media is about people, not insects. The Cicada Program, over 1,000 years old and tied to Japan and China, was run by Mei Wah, a host of Lilith who is now gone. Its Cicada 3301 puzzles recruit exceptionally intelligent children, who are placed in power centers worldwide, from the NSA and Pine Gap to politics, militaries, intelligence agencies, universities, and national laboratories. Phrases or jingles in a set order and frequency trigger subconscious orders, so Mei Wah could take over the world at a moment's notice. Yet the Cicadas, possibly numbering in the hundreds of thousands or millions, mostly believe they simply attended a school for gifted children; they are logical, capable people who could put their skills to positive use, and Kim is concerned for their safety.

Using a partial database obtained in Osaka, and encouraged by a fake money depository that appeared about 1,008 years after the program began, the SSP tried to activate the Cicadas and kidnapped some for access codes. As with the old Trustee Program (two keys in, three out, the third held by Marduk), the final key was Mei Wah's; it is now in Kim's system along with the master key, so the Cicadas can never be activated for a negative purpose. Operatives then tried to trigger a backup system at four sites, from New Zealand to a former NSA center under Maryland, before midnight China time on May 15, using Kim's stolen Consciousness and essence as a key. It failed, and all four sites were cleared that morning. Access requires 186 authentication factors and dual keys held by Kim and Source; either can block unauthorized access. Kim advises Cicadas who know they are in the program to hide for a week or two. The date ties to "M-E-I," the spelling of May in the Presidential Red Book, from the era when JFK, involved with the Black Sun, signed with Sukarno, then the Black Dragon, for Black Eagle Trust gold that created the $3 bill.

Countries, including BRICS members, are turning on China over missed payments and worthless bonds. China, which recently traded Mei Wah information on the Cicada Program with the SSP, reached out to Kim indirectly through Sim Sim. She refused: after the $1.479 trillion in U.S. debt she paid off and the 2012/2013 funding they misused, there are no talks until she receives her 2%, which could come as goods for C.A.R.E., from Apple products to vehicle chassis, but nothing if they launch the Bird Flu. Russia, behind on gas and oil payments, owes 2% of roughly $500 billion as well.

The clearing of All-That-Is continues. The Celestials are aspects of Source: nine each for Source, Anti-Source, and Neutral Source, governing timelines and Ages through domains such as the Tree of Death, Dark Essence, Dark Consciousness, and Dark Matter. As they rejoin Source, Dark, Light, and Neutral timelines and Ages give way to a single Golden Age, paving the way for Restoration: funding, C.A.R.E., and new food chains and transportation.

Kim closes with the "what ifs": had funding come earlier, with the people then in Assemblies and projects, allocations could have been squandered and projects handed to others. Source knows best, and one closed door opens another. The Deep State can get on the bus or get off the planet; Langley 5 and its SSP partners made promises they cannot keep. UNN app subscriptions via PayPal are now in beta.
