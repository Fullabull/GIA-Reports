Summary

Kim opens the June 26, 2024 report with an update on additional sites linked to the neurological disturbances discussed on Monday. Most have now been disabled, including locations from Sweden and Okinawa to Mount Shasta. Kim says the mechanisms at these sites were predominantly built from alien technology and had been operating under standing orders. Without that technology, she says the Deep State cannot recreate something of this magnitude.

On banking, Kim says operatives are keeping banks busy chasing claims that there is still gold somewhere that has never been used in the financial system. At the same time, they are pushing digital cash that has to remain outside the banking system, making it useless to banks as normal liquidity or assets. Fixers working for the Families -- the genetic shareholders of banks -- have arrived in Durango from JPMorgan Chase and Bank of New York Mellon after preliminary talks with JPMorgan's potential interim chairman. Kim says banks can survive by becoming service providers rather than custodians, and that her side would require a seat on the board as part of any agreement to help fix them.

The Deep State had also expected something to happen at sundown the night before, but nothing did. After that failed expectation, Megvii tried to install its Great Reset social crediting system more broadly. Kim says it cannot work without the enormous computing power once provided by Omega, Starlight (a Lucifer program), or Omega Project Stargate. She then explains the Farms, groups of highly trained Black Ops operatives bred, medically enhanced, and conditioned for extreme loyalty to individual presidents. Each U.S. president since the 22nd had a farm except Biden. Their accounts held liens and Money Four (M4) derivatives tied to assets and resources gained during a presidency. A recent attempt to gain access to the Farm Accounts and Farm Monetary System ended with the farm systems, fake accounts, black-ops monetary systems, and Langley backdoors being removed.

Kim closes with July 4, the next date the Deep State is expecting money. Under the old system, the major annual Alpha allocations funded governments around the world and were routed through the Federal Reserve to HSBC in Hong Kong for trading until government budgets came due. The last major July 4 allocation went out in 2012, sent by Kim herself. A large 2018 allocation was later pulled back, and a small U.S.-only allocation followed in 2019, but none are coming now. Kim says every major asset has already been leveraged, and she is content to keep giving the Deep State things to chase if it means they leave her side alone.
