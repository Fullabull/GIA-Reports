Summary

Kim opens the May 20, 2024 report by setting the record straight on Iran. The helicopter crash reported to have killed Iranian President Ebrahim Raisi and his staff was staged; they are alive and were located that morning in Belize. The setup begins the transfer of Iran from China's Order of the Dragon to the Pallavicini family. Khamenei, head of the Order of the Green Dragon, is a masked replacement for the long-dead original, and the president was in on the plan. A bankrupt China agreed to give up Iran for $5.5 trillion, with the next payment due Friday; Khamenei would be next to "die."

The Pallavicini family, now led by a man of about 40, had the Order of the Black Dragon find Mei Wah's old master keys to Omega, and obtained Marduk's master keys from the Order of the Dragon, which knew they did not work. The deal splits planet Earth 65% to the Pallavicini family and 35% to the Order of the Dragon. They have promised $75.42 trillion to countries including Russia and South Africa, probably China and the UK as well, but nothing to the United States. Instead, $56 billion went to Langley 5, U.S. operatives, and SSP people, including the Q-Crew and Trump operatives, to let the U.S. burn and then manage North America. The Q-Crew's "Iran is out, Egypt is in" post a day ahead of the crash tells Kim they took the money.

The blueprint is Marduk's decades-old Seven Currencies of the World Plan: seven centers each for the BIS, WTO, IMF, and UN under one umbrella, with military and intelligence control as well as finance. China would take Asia from India east; Russia, Europe; South Africa, Africa; Brazil, South America; the U.S., North America; and the UK, the Commonwealth, with William installed after Charles. The Middle East has passed from the GCC to Iraq to Iran, possibly under a new Shah; the former Shah was a Pallavicini. Seven base currencies, backed by the IMF SDR or gold, would follow a global crash. It cannot work: Marduk never released a plan without its final key, and his Alpha access ran through the Parents and the twins called Cassandra, who are gone.

Launched three days before the Full Moon, the attempts are exposing the last hidden control systems for Kim to clear. She shut down a reality-altering program used against targeted people, identified the attackers through the Key Intelligence and Military System, and watched them hit Command and Control, the global transfer platform, and the Grass bandwidth network; she expects them spent by Friday's payment date. Meanwhile, weekend C.A.R.E. meetings readied her side to move quickly: Tier One bank operating systems have merged with the Global Repository's, Tiers Two through Four are underway, and transfers now take about 10 minutes.

Kim exposes two Looking Glass lies: the June 13th concession date was actually 2012, and the claim that she would never drop a dollar. She names the Orsinis as the family slated to control the U.S. and the Anti-Silent Circle as still on the warpath. She closes by urging people to walk away from those who keep lying to them; your word is the one thing they cannot take.
