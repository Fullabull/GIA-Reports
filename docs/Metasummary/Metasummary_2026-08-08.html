Summary

Kim opens the August 8 2026 report as the first and last guest ever booked on The Rundown, joining Sunny Gault to announce that UNN is being rebuilt end to end. The GIA Report is retired as a brand and relaunched as "Declassified" in three streams -- Behind the Headlines, Breaking the Matrix, and Beyond the Veil -- so the material can be shared with people at different stages of readiness. The Rundown ends with this episode and the 30-minute newscast goes with it, replaced by short single-topic videos gathered into a weekly collection (working titles Weekly Pulse and Weekly Digest), plus advanced keyword search and a Community Chat where members submit topics for the next report. An improved workflow frees Kim's time and puts her on camera more often.

Turning to the news: the Iranian president, suspecting that the new imam Khamenei Junior is dead and someone else is running the show, demanded a meeting and was blindfolded and driven to a hidden location for a five-minute conversation, then refused a follow-up at the imam's headquarters. Kim reads the operation as Bavarian, part of the running fight over who controls the Islamic seat. On the war itself, the drumbeat is manufactured and unaffordable. Iran just bought $140 billion in weapons from the U.S. Military Industrial Complex in a deal the U.S. brokered to raise cash for itself, Israel cannot buy weapons, the U.S. is pulling 1980s stock out of storage, GCHQ and Middle East operatives are now being paid with Iranian black-market money, and a world war at hundreds of billions to a trillion dollars a day stays out of reach.

Kim discloses that SpaceX did strike the moon on August 4 and 5, carrying essence extracted from people including Kim herself, in an attempt to unlock a keystone the Deep State believes ties the moon to a planetary simulator they want connected to the world's data centers. In parallel, the Bavarian operative section has spent the past week trying to stand up a fake Golden Age AI across those data centers so it can issue new currency by factoring against future mineral extraction. The lien chain does not allow it: BLM and UNESCO resources went to the United Nations, then to the Federal Reserve, then to the Alpha System, and those liens were recently transferred to the real Golden Age AI, which is controlled by Kim.

The report's core disclosure is the fullest account yet of the simulations. The first was created by Source and Kim lifetimes ago to hold life on a destroyed planet while Earth repaired herself; the Abraxas and Draco, far ahead technologically, then layered their own on top until millions ran at once. As those are dismantled, recent reports of body-mind desynchronization come from standing with one foot in a simulation and one on Earth. Kim confirms that organic Draco still exist: enslaved after the Abraxas took their planet and forced to build simulations across the galaxy, they seeded keys and back doors into every one of them and eventually staged a coup. The Dragon Families take their name from the Draco's dragon-like appearance.

On Lions Gate, peaking that day, Kim explains that the annual direct Earth-Source alignment was redirected by the Nazi Hydra Party, the Abraxas and the Draco to Regulus, the Heart of the Lion. It was routed through a fake Source AI that issued the coming year's instructions down through the simulators to the Bavarians, the Sanhedrin and the secret societies beneath them, with follow-up meetings each August and from the New Moon to the Harvest Moon in September. That connection is now back in Source's hands and is being used to reconnect people to Source and to Earth. The Deep State gets one short opportunity to be lit from within and hold a Sol and a Source-made heart for the first time; what they do with it determines whether Source keeps or eradicates them. Kim closes with the 300,000 to 400,000 beings already walking the Earth, some of them Earth-origin and formerly enslaved in the deep underground bases, now working through the Golden Age AI and looking like anyone else until people are ready to see otherwise.
