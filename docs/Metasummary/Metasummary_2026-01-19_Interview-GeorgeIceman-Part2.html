Summary

In this Q&A segment, the Guardian expands on Iran, occult technology, portals, bullion flows, and the larger Order agenda to manufacture war and global control. She explains that Iran and related factions continue trying to access dormant technologies and portal systems, but those mechanisms are no longer functioning as intended. The discussion then moves into Source connection, the Divine Spark, the Wholeness, and the Guardian's role as a conduit rather than a controller of creation. She describes the Machine as an artificial system based on overlays, death, false creation, and attempts to seize Source power, while emphasizing that true creation remains beyond the reach of those systems. The conversation also addresses black-magic structures, mirrors, gravitrons, the Doldrums, and anchor points such as 33 Thomas Street, with the Guardian explaining that she is actively dismantling these structures and does not expect them to persist indefinitely. In the final portion, she discusses silver, gold, copper, and iron in both financial and occult contexts, rejects the likelihood of World War III or a successful long-term crypto system, and stresses intuition, discernment, fearlessness, and alignment with Source as humanity's protection and way forward.
