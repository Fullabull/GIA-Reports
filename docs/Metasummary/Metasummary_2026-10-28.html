Summary

Kim opens the October 28, 2026 report with Tank, continuing from Part One on October 26, by examining faith and its connection to the Financial System. She catalogs misguided faith: in money backed by a government's good faith, in gold-backed cash without knowing what the gold actually is, in presidents (Orange People included), in militaries whose upper ranks exist to protect Deep State assets, in doctors and health care systems, in religious leaders who are part of the Machine, and in science -- all telling people what they cannot do. That faith connects people to the Machine and keeps them in the Matrix. Fear replaces faith, and Tank names the CDC, the United Nations, and the FDA, whose pandemic directives doctors followed without question.

Faith in the Natural way can never be taken, only given away. Because the Primordials seeded the Machine, it carries their seed in place of the Seed of Source: the seed of doubt. Faith in Source is a knowing present in everyone, surfacing as intuition and sudden solutions; when Kim's water failed on her birthday, Source told her the fix was an O-ring. Tank cites a 2019 AstraZeneca trial product that left people feeling disconnected from God, and the FunVax, which turned the brain's response to scripture from connection with God into disgust; Kim reports similar disconnection, infertility, and anxiety from the HPV vaccine.

Knowing is the Wisdom. Source tells Kim when to stay and when to go, but she still has to act, and doubt pulls people back into dependency on the Matrix -- Source could have freed everyone long ago had all listened. Tank adds that a collective resonance of Homo sapiens with Source would be too powerful to contain, and that the connection to Source is the real gold backing the system. That connection is not the silver cord, which ties people to the Machine; it is unseen and within, like Wi-Fi -- the knowingness that is the Source Factor-X, which the Deep State can never predict. Kim intends to withhold information that could help the Deep State, which is about to be denied what it wants for the first time in forever.

Kim closes with the payoff: the gold that backed the system was always yours -- the true Seed of Source. Unable to access it, the Deep State waits for people to access it and hand over information, which then takes the form of memory, and throws everything at intercepting an important thought or intuition. Part Three covers the Financial System and freeing yourself from the system.
