Summary

Kim opens the July 1, 2024 report with the Deep State's Fourth of July plans, all premised on money falling from the sky that day. They include groundwork for a war with China, laid starting over the weekend; false flags for Washington, D.C.; possible systems crashes; and a financial-system shutdown by month's end. She recounts a weekend meeting staged over an underground facility. By switching locations at the last second, she learned operatives need 20 to 25 minutes to regroup. Their photos, video, and analysis still cannot classify her.

That leads to government psychic programs (the Jedi program, Men Who Stare at Goats, LSD programs), built on information from hostile alien races. Enslaved aliens in the DUMBs, held under Marduk's Global Martial Law, passed along only what could be counteracted. Quantum computer programs slapped down anyone, trained or not, who grew too light or too dark. Ordinary people were also steered by gurus and by a New Age movement seeded by government programs. Organized religion, for all the good it teaches and does, can lock people into someone else's recipe for connecting to Source. Kim names ten now-dismantled facilities that transmuted Earth's energy into Dark energy: New Mexico, Louisiana, Finland, the Caspian Sea, Ecuador, Canberra, Ho Chi Minh City, Costa Rica, Cantabria, and under the Euphrates.

With those governing programs lifted, people can now develop their own abilities without the former backlash. Her guidance is to do you: other people's methods are recipes to adapt, and the "higher self" is your own Source-given Wisdom. Empty your cup, trust guidance even when it is unwelcome, refuse the victim role, and use discernment about what you connect to. She describes shielding while projecting decoy thoughts, such as a nursery rhyme, which defeated an eight-and-a-half-hour reading attempt in Frankfurt. She also explains the Deep State's long-held fear of three to seven people like her joining together. Part of her weekend went to tracing where the No Joy Program was still coming from.

She closes on July 4th. The expected release of over $100 trillion has not happened since 2012, when Kim made the last such release herself, so governments and operatives will be disappointed and will simply set a new date. What remains of the Deep State plans meetings beginning with the July 5 New Moon. They expect instructions directly from aliens now that the hierarchy above them is gone, but no one is left to give them.
