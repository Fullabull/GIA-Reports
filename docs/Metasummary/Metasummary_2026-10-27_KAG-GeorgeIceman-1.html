Summary

Kim joins George Iceman, host of the Reveal Report and a longtime researcher of the occult, for the October 27, 2026 broadcast. George raises two viral scares: an Axios report of unauthorized AI agents inside customer accounts at major U.S. banks, and a video predicting a Friday bank collapse and a forced switch to digital currency. Kim explains that AI agents connect through MCP under permissions the bank controls; an agent cannot roam bank servers or crash a bank. The scare is a Deep State narrative, the same "die every two weeks, get rich every two weeks" cycle that tracks the moon.

The collapse was set for Friday, October 30, just ahead of All Hallows' Eve, when occultists believe they hold the most power over humanity, whom the inverted Financial System counts as the dead. On October 26 the factions went to DEFCON Zero and tried to transfer the Financial System to a backup system in Texas once run by the Federation military. Kim says this was probably their fourth attempt in a year to regain control of the Financial System. Nobody tested it first; it has not functioned in three or four years. Kim was held on standby until nine o'clock. Had the transfer worked, they would have demanded she concede. She alone never has, and the piece of Source she guards is what they want and cannot take.

Kim discloses that prisoner bonds are the base root asset of the Financial System. Decades of privatization turned citizens into numbered assets whose guaranteed purchases, the Worldwide Consumer Network, are factored at banks. Earth itself is a prison planet: bonds issued against every person in perpetuity keep humanity in debtors' prison, taxes are the coupons, and every bond is registered with ANNA in Belgium, partner of the X/N System, the worldwide tax authority. Bonds for existence run up to $5.9 million a day per citizen. Money and crypto are debt notes, crypto the "currency for the dead," and the new crypto platform has never run two minutes. The Clintons' Evergreen Foundation failed to gain control of the Financial System in the 1980s and '90s. The Federation, the Sanhedrin, and a separate soulless rebel faction called the Foundation, which Kim will cover in the next GIA Report, all fight over these bonds, but none can reissue them; without them the Elite lose the right to leverage people or land, including Greenland.

Kim closes with reassurance: Source always steps in. Use discernment, hang in there, and talk to God.
