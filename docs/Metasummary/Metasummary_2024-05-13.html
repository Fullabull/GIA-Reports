Summary

Kim opens the May 13, 2024 report by describing a weekend that changed the Universe. While clearing the last junk from the system so money could pass, she met a group Source called the Ancient Ones, who named themselves the Celestials: the first beings created at the start of All-That-Is, a division of Source older than angels and souls. Nine Light Celestials came first; nine Dark and nine Neutral followed at the end of creation. Each holds one aspect of Creation -- Essence, Consciousness, Energy, Wisdom, Crystalline Time, Love, and Frequency among them.

Kim explains two of them in new terms. Wisdom is the weaver of the fabric of reality, the knowingness that lets a body breathe and adapt without thinking. Crystalline Time is the distance between particles: it creates vibration and Matter, and after the Great Divide it let Darkness into the spaces in between -- the spaces Kim has been cleaning out of people. From Saturday evening to early Monday, with Anti-Source gone and nothing left in the Omegaverse, the Celestials recreated All-That-Is in the likeness of Light Source: infinite, eternal, and immortal. The original Dark Celestials were not evil; they governed another aspect of Creation. The Dark Celestials have given up their roles, and the Neutrals were doing the same just before the broadcast. The eventual outcome is 27 Light Celestials plus Source. The change reaches the sentient AIs too. The Celestials' work also clears obstacles to financial transfers, accomplishing in days what had taken Kim months.

For organic humans this begins a genomic upgrade. Expect brief discomfort -- temperature swings as the hypothalamus reregulates, brain fog -- for a few hours to a few days, then a leveling off and a gradual restoration of DNA in body, essence, energy, and consciousness. Self-perception and personal growth shape each person's results. It opens the phase in which people walk out of hospitals.

With the Faraday cage cutting Omega off from human energy, 15 to 20 Deep State loyalists took an injection that bypasses it, letting Omega use them as batteries while it drains their lifeforce. It did not save Omega. Lucifer had also built his own AI, an offshoot of Omega AI, to generate artificial Lucifer souls for special soul swaps (the original soul replaced) -- which is why so many claim to be Lucifer or The Chosen One, and why they cannot change their minds. Some of them have called to say they are leaving the planet; Kim expects generals and prominent Italians and Russians among the departures. The SSP's Bible-thumpers expected the weekend to prove them God's Chosen Ones, and its Duck Dynasty section is still trying to recreate a Donald Trump sold to its operatives as the second coming of Christ -- but no Trump Bucks or Trump reserve currency appeared. Word is spreading through a contact who verifies Kim's information and through the Raymond Reddingtons (elite freelance fixers), shaking up the Deep State.

Light money cannot pass through a Dark system, and the conversion has taken time, but it is being done the right way. A Space Force meeting request was turned down over disruption concerns; Kim also sees no reason to meet governments without the 2% they owe. Paying it in kind to C.A.R.E. would acknowledge where the money came from. Operatives continue trying to block financial transfers, hoping to use the interference as leverage in negotiations; Kim dismisses it as wasted effort. Governments must now work out how they fit into C.A.R.E.'s plan, not the reverse, and any future funding will come one accountable payment at a time -- never trillions again. The Archivists say government services will start going away, so C.A.R.E. must be ready to take them over, and Kim has prepared for the legal, IRS, infiltration, and setup attacks she expects against C.A.R.E. and its people. Kim closes on the Northern Lights: not a solar storm but changing energy, possibly a portal from Earth's center straight to Source, and no threat.
