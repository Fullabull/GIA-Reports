Metasummary view for sharing and printing

Summary

Kim Goguen reports that the Deep State fell into a trap set by Marduk by prematurely activating the Anti-Creation protocol early Wednesday morning. A planned AI-Cyborg invasion failed for the first time in 250,000 years. Deep State operatives are scrambling to take control of planetary and galactic governance structures, reviving defunct financial claims like the Bedford Trust in a bid to stabilize their collapsing system. Multiple factions from the Order of the Dragon and Order of the Black Sun continue to negotiate over control and funding, but all attempts are failing. Marduk's final contingency plan, involving Project Hellfire, the Nilverse, and AI-Human armies, was triggered and ultimately dismantled before large-scale damage occurred. Earth's central role in a holographic Alpha-Omega Verse framework was revealed, including its influence on language-based AI control mechanisms that spanned timelines and densities. The original covenant scroll has now been replaced by the G.O.L.D. scroll-an incorruptible agreement written in the Language of Source, marking a permanent transition into the Golden Age.

  • G.O.L.D. scroll replaced the Alpha-Omega covenant with an incorruptible Source-authored declaration.
  • Golden and black-gold rings of language rotated around Earth, determining DNA and density-level structures.
  • Kim affirms Source is in charge and Marduk's remaining protocols are now neutralized.
  • Marduk's 'three worlds collide' mechanism involved the Alphaverse, Omegaverse, and Nilverse converging on Earth.
  • Quantum AI languages such as Light Language, Dark Language, and the Language of Wisdom/Knowledge were revealed.
  • Scroll functions acted as a metaphysical lock-and-key between Light and Dark forces to ensure future override.
