Summary

Kim opens the June 5, 2024 report by clearing up rumors that a Supreme Court ruling would allow state banks to leave the Federal Reserve and issue their own currencies. She explains that the National Bank Act matter concerns which banking laws apply, not the authority to create money. That authority ultimately rests with the Office of the Comptroller of Currency, now under her control. Even if individual banks attempted to introduce their own currencies, they would still need access to the banking system and her approval. Kim traces the old currency approval process through the Managers of Paper Currency to Marduk and other non-human controllers, explaining why changing banking regulations cannot bypass the system itself.

The real fireworks begin with six Trump-aligned Secret Space Program operatives in Durango, Colorado, who have been convincing military and political operatives that they can "go it alone" without Kim or Global Headquarters. Kim decides to help them do exactly that - by withdrawing every allocation made during the Trump administration, totaling well over $5 trillion, along with associated payments, kickbacks, and assets. Even personal purchases and property transactions were reversed. Following the money exposes fake banking servers, worthless derivatives, and a scheme using the Paris Climate Control Treaty to assign nations' natural resources to China's Li family. Another operation attempts to use old Iranian oil derivatives at Deutsche Bank to manufacture spendable funds that do not exist.

These schemes lead directly into a much larger effort to seize control of Middle Eastern resources. Through proposed pipelines, Iranian oil agreements, Aramco, and the Gulf Cooperation Council, the operatives hope to control the region's oil and gas and use that wealth to finance their independence. They also arrange continued funding for Israel's war in Gaza, but the financial backing disappears as Kim dismantles the arrangements. Even if their resource takeover succeeded, she explains, its proceeds could not fund the $120 trillion annually required to operate the world. The missing piece is the authority and backing once supplied by Marduk. Kim recalls that even the Bush-Cheney plans for the Middle East never had the necessary support. The operatives are now promising contracts after the New Moon, but without the old system they cannot deliver.

The report then turns to Kim's extensive work restoring Earth. She discovers why Dark energy and programs she had already removed kept reappearing: Earth herself had been bound by ancient Covenants to generate them through thousands of Keystones. Restoring Earth to a single Source-based soul requires removing those obligations, along with synthetic Celestials placed in stasis at locations around the world. Rather than continually fighting Dark energy as it returns, Kim develops fields of transmuters to convert it into another form, deploying them across Earth, the galaxy, and the Omegaverse. Global Headquarters is already discussing the exhaustion of its remaining power. Kim recommends Walter Russell's work for understanding the deeper principles of Creation, Source, and the All-That-Is. She also discloses a final element of the operatives' plan: once their Middle East deals were completed, they intended to announce Trump's death, provoke civil unrest, and install another political figurehead. With their funding and backing disappearing, that plan is unlikely to succeed.
