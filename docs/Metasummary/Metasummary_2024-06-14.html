Summary

Kim opens the June 14, 2024 report discussing the anxiety she experienced overnight and the repeated technical interruptions during the broadcast. Turning to financial news, Russia's efforts to move away from the U.S. dollar through XRP or a new BRICS currency are going nowhere. Kim explains that the world's payment systems were tied to the Omega system, and the countries attempting to replace them lack the technology to do so. The Global Repository now operates on Codex 685, far beyond the Codex 60 technology humans had reached. Saudi Arabia is also looking to reduce its dependence on the dollar. Kim explains how international trade creates demand for currencies, and how shifting oil and other commodity sales into different currencies could weaken demand for the dollar.

The morning's "hack-o-rama" was an attempt by the Rothschild family, Global Headquarters, and Langley 5 to launch Marduk's Seven Currencies of the World Plan during the G7 meeting. But the Deep State had changed the original plan. The Amero was designed for North America, yet they promised it to South America as well. They also divided Europe into two currency regions instead of one. These changes caused the programs to fail, revealing that all seven currencies and their underlying assets would ultimately have remained under Marduk's control. With Marduk gone, only Kim could authorize the final step, and she deleted the systems. The Amero, the GCC currency, Azure, and the proposed Eastern European, African, and Australian currencies all failed. The Deep State even offered Kim a percentage of the global asset pool, apparently unaware that she already controlled 100% of it. Her intention is to distribute those assets for the benefit of humanity, creating jobs, increasing production, and introducing better technologies.

Kim then reveals the source of the unusual anxiety. Anu, Marduk's grandfather, had created a system of 10,368,000 artificial replicas of Earth, one for every timeline, using copies of Earth's original DNA blueprint for the Universe. These uninhabited replicas allowed the Abraxas and others to manipulate Earth's elements, energy, and etherical field across different Ages. A reflective prism field around Source captured Source's energy, essence, and consciousness, interfering with Source's ability to send them outward to Creation. The transmuter fields Kim installed around humans the previous Saturday created an energy shortage that triggered these dormant replicas, which still responded to Dark energy. Kim eliminated the replicated Earth system and is considering reverse engineering the cloaking technology that hides other dormant systems. She also developed a new Global Repository point-of-sale system and converted replicated SWIFT and IBAN systems to Codex 685, operating on Lifi, to improve the speed and efficiency of financial transactions.

Kim also discusses reports of a planned assassination attempt against Donald Trump in Pennsylvania on June 13, which she describes as another sympathy campaign, along with an earlier program that triggered feelings of sadness and thoughts of death or suicide. She advises people experiencing sudden, disturbing thoughts or emotions to ask themselves, "Is this my thought?" and take time to ground themselves, breathe, and regain perspective before acting. She expects no major announcements from the G7 and closes with Father's Day wishes.
