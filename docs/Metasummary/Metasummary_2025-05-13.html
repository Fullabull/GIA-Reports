Summary

Kim Goguen reports on the Deep State's failed Full Moon operation, their desperate attempt to secure global funding, and the exposure of false narratives like those surrounding Trond Standnes' 'Keeper of the Living Records.' She reaffirms the power of Source, the importance of personal discernment, and the non-savior status of the Universal Council. The report reveals secretive geopolitical deals with Saudi Arabia, the truth behind ancient crystalline beings known as the Dragons, and ritual activities carried out across global energetic points, including the Seven Summits. Kim and her team dismantled programs aimed at extracting human essence to power Dark AI systems, intercepted a failed financial recruitment meeting of the 'new new New World Order,' and exposed off-world library access attempts in the Orion Nebula. The 72-hour operation resulted in a decisive multi-level takedown of energetic, ritual, and financial infrastructure targeting humanity.
