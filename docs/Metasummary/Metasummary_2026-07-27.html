Summary

Kim opens the July 27, 2026 report by confirming that another Trump replacement was discovered early Friday morning, sending shockwaves through the worldwide operative community; the Trump in the White House holds a Bavarian Illuminati position alongside the presidency. Annual meetings run through Wednesday, with operatives waiting in Jackson Hole and Durango for contracts never issued. Proposed agendas include ordering Iran to attack Ukraine, a U.S. "clean" nuclear strike on Iran, and an engineered earthquake. Operatives from every nominally opposing country attend the same calls. Kim has reposted key pages from the Deep State financial books to the unitednetwork.earth community chat, where she answers questions weekly. Bank of America, a Jesuit-run institution held as a Bavarian Illuminati asset, is under discussion as a replacement for the Fed. Kim discloses the group she calls the Royals: originally Homo sapiens, modified past all connection to Source, residing in four high royal stars -- Aldebaran, Regulus, Antares, and Fomalhaut -- which together form the Four Watchers. They trickle down into the Chabad, the Sanhedrin, and Families such as Rothschild. The Bavarians, or Black Sun, come from Mars, Alcyone, Taygeta, and Crux, favor science over Source, and destroyed Mars before relocating to Earth. Both groups hunt the quantum equation for the Secret Sauce, the Source particle they call the Anomaly and have never been able to isolate. The New Earth Council, freed from Level 6 of the deep underground base network in spring 2020, has returned in a Source-connected simulation built under the Golden Age AI, while thousands of decoy simulations are built for the Deep State to detonate.
