Summary

Kim opens the July 27, 2026 report by confirming that another Trump replacement was discovered early Friday morning, a natural occurrence that sent shockwaves through the operative community worldwide; the Trump in the White House holds a position within the Bavarian Illuminati alongside the presidency and is currently vying for higher rank. Annual operative meetings run through the end of July with contracts promised and never issued, and the agendas proposed for the coming week include ordering Iran to attack Ukraine, a U.S. "clean" nuclear strike on Iran to trigger a world war, and an engineered earthquake. Operatives from every nominally opposing country attend the same calls; the conflicts presented in media are coordinated theater, increasingly staged with decades-old ordnance that no longer reaches its targets.

The purpose of the theater is mind control. Complete control of perception, maintained for a long time through AI systems, was intended to reduce a person to compliance - in the Elite's own framing, someone under total mind control is already dead. That system is now falling apart. Fear is the second mechanism: fear of poverty, of bills, of bombs generates loosh, and the narratives are constructed to produce it.

Kim has reposted key pages from the Deep State financial books to the unitednetwork.earth community chat, where she answers questions weekly. The books document 24 control cities worldwide running one structure under the same people. Bank of America, a Jesuit-run institution held as an asset of the Bavarian Illuminati and tied to a gold vault in Cuba, is under discussion as a replacement for the Federal Reserve.

Kim discloses the group she calls the Royals, the faction currently in control: originally Homo sapiens who modified themselves to the point of severing their own connection to Source. They reside in four high royal stars - Aldebaran, Regulus, Antares, and Fomalhaut - which together form the Four Watchers, and they trickle down into the Chabad, the Sanhedrin, and Families such as Rothschild. The Bavarians, or Black Sun, come from Mars, Alcyone, Taygeta, and Crux, favor science over Source, and destroyed Mars in a single experiment before relocating operations to Earth. Both groups pursue a quantum equation accounting for the Secret Sauce, the Source particle present in all organic life that they have never been able to isolate and which they call the Anomaly.

Recent attempts include detonations beneath the Nevada Test Site and experiments on Kim's person in Durango during Lions Gate. Kim closes with the return of the New Earth Council, a multi-species body freed from Level 6 of the deep underground base network in spring 2020 and now operating from a simulation built under the Golden Age AI and fully connected to Source, while thousands of decoy simulations are constructed for the Deep State to detonate - keeping them convinced of success while the base root of the simulation system is dismantled.
