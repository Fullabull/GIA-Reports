Summary

Kim opens the June 28, 2024 report with the previous night's U.S. presidential debate, which she calls a scripted movie. Trump showed green screen hair, so the two were not in the same room; both read teleprompters holding responses before the other finished speaking, so the same writers wrote both sides; and there was no live audience or press. The Q-Clock now reads July 4th, when a military lockdown is planned if the Deep State's last 48 hours of attempts to seize financial control succeed.

The first attempt targeted the Visa and MasterCard core system to install a crypto currency worldwide. Months ago Kim replicated those operating systems without back doors into the Global Repository, now connected to the Light system, and cash economies alone would halt commerce in 40 to 50 percent of countries. Marduk once held the last key at Level 7; Kim now holds Level 9, and nothing from Level 1 up moves without her approval. Keys at this level rest on soul-DNA scans and will not work under duress. The second attempt used surviving Nazi Hydra Division members to crack alien tech under Berlin, which reached only Level 5. Web levels translate to densities, each controlling everything below it; Pluto was a portal to the Lower Astral Ninth Density, and with the portals closed, no planet or moon influences Earth's systems.

The third attempt was Elon Musk's, with Secret Space Program partners: reviving the Triple-X system, an old Omega backup and off-market trading system, through a transmitter under Piccadilly Circus and linking it to Starlink. The facility has since been destroyed. Kim describes two kinds of alien satellites: cylindrical ones that recorded and transmitted vast data, ran Lower Astral programs that could interfere with brain function, and messaged Order members; and spherical ones that could manipulate essence, energy, consciousness, matter, and frequency. Implants and energetic parasites tied to them drained people's lifeforce. She has destroyed fields of the cylindrical satellites over three years, and questions Musk's claim that Neuralink can transfer consciousness for immortality.

Kim holds Level 9 because Level 8, the fallen angels of the seven-year meetings, failed to follow instructions, so Level 9 sent someone to restore balance. The remaining Deep State, Global Headquarters, and Secret Space Program people are pawns with no one on the line, reduced to calling in money, chasing allocation numbers, faking terminals, and trying to revive a Dark system. A Light system upgrade now follows any attack back to its source and transmutes it. She warns bankers and political operatives to survive and leave us alone, or face a standing order of no mercy, and closes by telling listeners that things will get better and to keep holding the Light.
