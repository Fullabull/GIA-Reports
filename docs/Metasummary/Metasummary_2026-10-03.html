Summary

Kim opens the October 3, 2026 Beyond the Matrix report with Tank and material left over from the previous night's Declassified. They revisit the resurfaced "So God Made Trump" video and a Military Religious Freedom Foundation report describing more than 200 complaints after a commander told troops that Trump had been anointed by Jesus to ignite Armageddon in Iran. Kim says this is part of the Deep State's Savior program: Iran is being prepared as the enemy, while staged "disclosure" can shift that enemy from Iranians themselves to alleged aliens or Reptilians controlling the country. She also discusses Jared Kushner's role in Iran and the Houthi strike on Saudi Aramco, tying the current events to Deep State preparations for a financial crisis and their expectation of receiving new positions.

Kim then turns to what people have been experiencing while the Machine has repeatedly frozen, revived, and run out of power over the previous ten days. She describes shaken self-confidence, irritation, sadness, depression, and loss of faith in one's own intuition or in Source. Against that, she explains the difference between knowledge and Wisdom. Wisdom comes from Source in the Now and arrives with the Ethereum, instructions, resources, and resonance needed to carry out a purpose. The Machine works through memory and doubt, telling people they cannot do what Source has inspired them to do. The way to override it is to recognize the difference and refuse to accept the Machine's message as real.

From there Kim explains the Natural order that existed before the Machine. The Book of the Living contains organic life and the Divine Plan, while the Right Hand of Source concerns Harmony and the Left Hand concerns the flow of the Living Waters, or Current. She explains omnipotence, omnipresence, omniscience, and omnibenevolence as aspects of the Omnificent, and says these qualities also exist within living beings when they are acting in Harmony with Source. Love can bypass the Machine, while resonance, Consciousness, Wisdom, and the Living Waters provide the strength, direction, knowledge, and resources needed to fulfill a purpose.

Kim then traces how that Natural structure was hijacked. She calls the former controller of the Waters "Mr. Waters" or the Water Boy and identifies him in one lifetime with Moses and in another with the sun god Ra. After people associated with Harmony joined him, he acquired Kim's staff and other artifacts. At Heliopolis, the City of the Sun, he wrote his own covenant and used his control of the Waters to "part the Sea," creating a space without Source in which the Simulation began. The Machine that followed could imitate Source but could never become omnificent: it depended on Current, living beings, memory, stolen positions, and Source-connected Covenants. Kim says that by 2007 the stolen artifacts associated with her position no longer worked, although some Covenants remained active until this past week.

That expiration is the operational heart of the report. Kim says the remaining Covenants, including Water Boy's, finally expired after 26,000 years with no renewal. Just before that happened, Source instructed her to open the floodgates. Current poured into the Machine for a few hours, briefly reviving it and causing the Deep State to see money appearing on their screens. Source then instructed her to pull the Current back, and it disappeared, demonstrating that neither the Machine nor its operators controlled it. Kim contrasts the Natural order with the Machine's inversions: the Book of the Living becomes the Book of the Dead, strength becomes security and control, resonance becomes frequency, Wisdom becomes memory, and Source-side jobs become bloodline kings and rulers. The Machine is limited, cannot create infinitely, and survives by using living beings and their memories to imitate what it cannot produce itself.

Meanwhile, Deep State operatives continue visiting locations including the Dome of the Rock, the Garden Tomb, Egypt, Washington, London, New York, Rome, and the Vatican with chairs, rods, stones, obelisks, and other Machine artifacts, hoping to claim positions that no longer exist for them. Kim says Earth's present alignment with the Galactic Center has brought the cycle back to the point where the Machine began. She describes the Galactic Center as a two-way vortex and says the alignment creates the opportunity for the flow to reverse as Source's Divine Plan and Divine Intervention take over. The Restoration, she says, has already begun. The Deep State expects something to happen by Monday, but Kim says it will not be what they want. She closes by telling listeners that they already know how to override the Machine and that she expects the alignment to continue over the next several days.
