Summary

Kim opens the June 10, 2024 report by explaining that an agreement governing control of the world's assets expired the day before. The Deep State had been counting on this expiration to gain control of valuable resources such as gold, oil, gas, and rare earth minerals - and because these commodities are traded in U.S. dollars, they hoped to use those assets to undermine the dollar and establish a new financial system under their control. They also planned to publicly announce Global Martial Law as part of this effort. But their attempt to seize control of the securities tied to those assets failed - the Alpha system, which previously managed the financial claims against them, had already transferred its operations to the Light System. The assets have now been divided into roughly eight billion portions, with the Global Repository holding them in trust for humanity - the people, not the Deep State, are now the beneficiaries of the world's assets.

Kim details the weekend's failures: helicopter after helicopter brought operatives expecting contracts that never came; attempts to connect BlackRock's Aladdin system and Citadel to the Treasury failed because the underlying technology no longer worked; and attempts to issue, extend, or replicate Treasury bonds, including bonds Kim assigned in 2022, also failed. She explains that standing orders from the 16,000-year-old Global Martial Law agreement under Marduk had also been reversed - orders that provided funding through war bonds before each Solstice to prevent the world from becoming too Light. The Financial System now operates under the Light System's Peace operating system, which no longer recognizes government or military bonds and Department of Defense vouchers.

Behind the scenes, the Federal Reserve may publicly acknowledge problems managing U.S. monetary policy, and five board members are negotiating retirement - while the Treasury, run by the Order of the Black Sun, prepares to take over the Fed's worldwide role. Meanwhile, BRICS believes it can create an alternative to the U.S. dollar within five years, and the Rothschild family, now under a new leader called Philip, is offering to run a currency for the Middle East. Separately, the Trump operatives are planning to use a legal loophole to overturn his 34 state convictions, keeping him available as a source of revenue through November.

But none of these financial plans addresses the underlying problem - the old operating systems used by BlackRock and Vanguard depended on alien technology that is no longer available. Without it, those systems cannot handle the enormous volume of transactions required for a worldwide financial network, especially as countries push toward digital payments while much of the world's population still relies on cash.

Kim warns that governments may not survive, leaving millions of people without familiar services such as food assistance, health care, Social Security, and pensions. And because people are no longer being leveraged as government assets, the old financial arrangements that supported those systems are disappearing. Essentials and Life Assurance are intended to help fill the gap, but replacing government services takes time - even creating specialized payment card networks requires extensive programming. Meanwhile, financial institutions are being converted to Codec 685 and an asset-based system, while the Marketplace, now in beta testing, is being developed into something much larger - a platform for children's education with rewards, adult vocational training, agricultural trading, and access to professional advice and services. Kim closes by explaining that progress in transmuting Darkness into Light still fluctuates as the Deep State continues trying to manifest its plans - and encourages Light workers to remain positive, spend time with family, and use their own creative energy to help counteract those efforts.
