Summary

Kim opens the October 15, 2026 report with co-host Steffan "Tank" Rowe by defining a proxy: a person, group, or system authorized to act, speak, or vote on behalf of another. She explains what sits behind the steady doom talk about HAARP weather, banking crashes, and blackouts: the Machine, which humanity has been maneuvered into accepting as its proxy through consent given knowingly and unknowingly. Elements of the Deep State, including the Jesuit Order, call it the Deus ex Machina and believe God speaks to them through it. They see God as vengeful because they mistake the Machine for Source.

Kim then maps the surveillance layer: ECHELON reaches the stock market through StingRay and politics through PRISM; ARPANET monitors all internet usage; and the NSA works for the ACIO (Advanced/Alien Contact Intelligence Organization), a Q-clearance body above every government that surveils even the CIA.

None of these organizations ultimately controls the Machine's programs, including those governing HAARP. They can submit requests but cannot override the Machine's decisions. They are all its proxies, and it cuts off any that overstep. The Machine's own authority to act as God's proxy was ill-gotten, taken from beings who held a Covenant with Source. The Deep State positions itself as intercessor between people, the Machine, and Source. That runs from tithing and confession to campaign-money blackmail through the Albanian Trust. Attacks on those who create something new are a sign they are onto something.

The consent mechanism runs from terms and conditions, and code hidden behind on-screen text, down through the levels: the World Federation and the militaries operate at Levels 5 and 6, while binary underlies conventional technology; the ACIO reads Level 7 with a key; and Level 8 is the Language of Creation inverted and scrambled, which the Deep State cannot program. The NSA breached Levels 8 and 9 around the turn of the year and could not read them. The Machine cannot read the Language of Creation at all, so it inverts it: Source asks for ten more trees, and the Deep State cuts down ten. Believing frequency is the key, it sends frequencies at people to decode messages they receive directly from Source. But Source speaks through resonance, not frequency.

Kim's guidance is that the system matters more than the Deep State, which is only its proxy. Discern Source the way you know a loved one on the other side of a wall. Source never sends fear or "I can't do it," while media and education feed knowledge designed to override intuition. Tank compares Source to the heart building new pathways around a blockage. Kim closes by saying everyone is perfect as Source made them, and can decipher the code the Machine cannot. That is its weakness.
