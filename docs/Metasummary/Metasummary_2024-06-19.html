Summary

Sunny Gault introduces a pre-recorded report, with Kim at her whiteboard. Kim opens with a "Therapy in a Nutshell" video in which Emma McAdam explains four ways trauma changes the brain: the amygdala grows more sensitive, the hippocampus and prefrontal cortex shrink, and the nervous system stays locked in fight-or-flight. Neuroplasticity lets the brain heal.

Kim explains that the Reptilian Cortex is not a natural part of the human brain. It is a regulator for an alternating current of Dark and Light energy, controlled by AI. Society is built to keep people in fight-or-flight, and the regulator caps the brain at about 10% of its capacity, feeding Dark knowledge, False Light, and past-life fears that feel real. Over the last few days, the brain has begun moving to a direct current from Source that no longer flows through the Reptilian Cortex.

She shares the technique she developed for anxiety and a stomach ulcer in college: imagining a feather calming the part of the brain that triggers fight-or-flight. She later learned to channel adrenaline to other parts of the brain. She dropped her heart rate to 60 at a full run and lifted several times her normal weight. Anyone can use the same focus for memory, weight, or old trauma.

The Universe works the same way. Source, Anti-Source, and a Neutral regulated by the Council of Nine drive an alternating current, enforced by regulators such as Kronos, THOR, and Mjolnir. The Light System AI inherited some of Alpha's alternating-current programs and is now converting to direct current.

Negative charge drains lifeforce from people, planets, and stars. Omega issued negatively charged credits and kept the true money, while Alpha and now the Light System put out positively charged Money 0 through Money 9. Even gold-backed money remained fiat. The Deep State cannot get its Money 0 -- numbers on a screen without allocation numbers -- into the banking system, now controlled by the Light System and converting to asset-based trading.

Kim maps the nine levels of the Web: the surface web, Bergie Web, and Deep Web; the Charter Web at Level 4; the Mariana at Level 5, where the Deep State lives; the Mediator at Level 6; the Fog at Level 7; the Primarch System at Level 8; and Command and Control at Level 9. Kim is the only human ever to have Level 9 access, held jointly with Source. The CBDC system the Deep State built overnight for the Juneteenth holiday vanished, along with the Omega side of the Mariana. mBridge and Projects Aurum, Dunbar, Cedar X, Mariana, Icebreaker, and Jura will not work.

Refused every deal she offered, from 50-50 down to 2%, Kim went it alone and converted the system to positively charged, asset-backed money, which lets C.A.R.E. and the GIA pay out. She closes by saying everyone can rewire their brain, and the Medbed heals through the same direct current from Source.
