Summary

Kim opens the September 16, 2026 report by continuing Beyond the Matrix, reporting that the full reconnection to Source, for people and for Earth, is harder than expected, though momentous and painful changes came over the past week. She begins with the light body and the Higgs boson field. The Higgs boson particle is what science calls the God particle and what she calls the Source particle; in a natural world that field surrounds everything and nothing separates us from Source. The Machine created an artificial Higgs boson field over the real one; the real field still exists, but is now divided into the colors we know -- that exist only in a Simulation. Those divisions are barriers that interfere with the direct connection between a person, their own Consciousness, and the Consciousness of Source, and they are why nothing is self-lit anymore: your energy, your essence, and your Consciousness no longer light themselves. The Divine Plan remains within every Source particle and every cell; the Machine works by intercepting, separating, and distorting the information carried through that natural connection.

Between the Planes of Existence sit floors and ceilings that make passage between Planes nearly impossible, and that control who moves where. A floor is the artificial Higgs boson field without the Source Juju, the part of the Divine Plan they have never been able to access; it looks like black obsidian covered in blue cobalt goo -- the Black Goo people talk about, which Kim identifies as the Database of Source Factor X and the MIND of its source. The Machine extracts information from your Divine Plan particle into a floor, where natural quantum entanglement keeps you tied to what sits there while the information is distorted, read, and passed down to "floor one," the basement. Nothing of you lives there. What lives there are the particles the Deep State deposits through its own rituals, and beings from beyond Earth -- what Kim calls the universal Deep State. The ceiling above holds every being on every planet. Information moves between the ceiling and the basement, is translated through the database Kim calls the God machine, a fake-Source machine, and is transmitted back into your body, your mind, and your soul. It travels in and out through controlled windows and doors, and the main windows on the planet can overlay and manipulate what is experienced in the Simulation: weather, chemtrails in the sky, the people around you, even a replacement president. The same architecture works through frequencies and emotions, because the colors can only reach a person through the emotions -- old traumas and buttons, dreams sent while you sleep, a store where everyone is suddenly hostile -- all of it designed to keep attention away from a direct connection with Source.

The windows themselves are physical. Kim describes one as a pane of glass painted black, with a small amount of the paint scraped away to let the gold beneath shine through, read in real time by laser lights passing back and forth like an old copy machine. They sit in the Middle East, Grenada, Palau, two in the United States for the eastern and western halves, London, the Netherlands and Liechtenstein, and across a third of the African continent and half of Europe. The main one for the planet, which Kim calls a Level 9 window, was created from her in the past. Over the last ten days the Deep State has tried to launch several systems through these windows and to connect grids to them, so that it keeps power and control even if the floor falls out from underneath it.

That effort runs on things taken from Kim. The Machine's combination of minerals came from her, including geranium and silica, along with supercrete, her replacement for concrete that helps rather than harms and has no 75-year half-life; the Machine built its consciousness around those minerals, which sit predominantly at seven supersites such as Silverton, Colorado and the Democratic Republic of Congo, each holding minerals worth hundreds of trillions of dollars. The Deep State knows of only a few. George "the Viking" submitted to Kim their plan for the Treasury Department to override the Fed and base a new financial system, QFS and otherwise, on those same assets. It cannot work. They still cannot read the Source X, Densities 9 through 12 are blocked to them and to the Machine, and the missing factor is who Kim is and what her job is, which is why she stopped writing things down and stopped talking about this technology on the internet.

The structure traces back to an ancient Covenant and Kim's Guardian role as a conduit for Harmony, a job she has held here for some 36,000 years. She appointed people to maintain Harmony on their respective planets, drawing through her the information needed to perform those jobs; all but one turned away from their original purpose and became part of the universal Deep State. They were the first people in the floor, and from them came the Bloodline Families, the Parents, and the operatives, presidents, and heads of state beneath them. Kim calls them the Rainbow folk: the makers of the colors, the shadow planes, and the emotions, and now out of work. Her own paracausal was extracted roughly 26,000 years ago and set into what looks like a crystal or diamond in the Middle East, which no longer functions; it is also why the gates cluster there, along with the Equinox point where all things are equal and Lions Gate. The Machine then used minerals, seven supersites, frequencies, and technology taken from her to reproduce and control parts of that structure, but it never possessed the complete Divine Plan or direct Source connection required to make the system whole. It can intercept, copy, and distort; it cannot create what originates from Source.

What changed this week is that the Covenant was not renewed. The Deep State expected it on the New Moon of the 11th, or in the days right after and no later than the 15th, and it did not come; they cannot renew it directly with Source. There is a new Covenant now and a new Divine Plan, the breakdown began on the 15th, and Kim is no longer obligated to those people in any way, shape, or form. People are more connected to their own Consciousness and the Consciousness of Source than ever before, which is why recent days have felt good and then suddenly not: they are still fighting it. Whatever is left of the Machine will not be functioning much longer. Meeting season runs until the Full Moon around the 26th, with the operatives on the ground waiting for their orders, their instructions, and the money that comes with them. Kim closes with what a person can do in the meantime. Love for yourself is not the thing the word has come to mean; it is respect for every cell in your body, and it is what reconnects those cells to each other. Frequency and sound healing can help do that work. Be your own conductor. As people and Earth reconnect directly with Source and their own Divine Plan, the Machine loses the mechanism through which it controls perception, emotion, and reality.
