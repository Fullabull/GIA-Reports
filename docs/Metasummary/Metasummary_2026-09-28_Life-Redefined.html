Summary

Kim opens the September 28 2026 Life Redefined report by introducing Karen Sherwood, a leadership educator and consultant with more than 30 years in human development and 16 years working with the science of differentiation, widely known as Human Design and applied in business through BG5. Rather than a conventional interview, Kim becomes the subject of her own annual cycle analysis, which she calls her "birthday report." Karen explains that the chart rests on birth date, time, and location -- the imprint of the neutrino stream at birth, not astrology. The analysis returns a person to their design, completes the lessons of the past year, and prepares for the cycle ahead. Part One covers the design itself.

Karen identifies Kim's career type as a Classic Builder whose process is step by step: waiting to respond, following her intuitive gut, and moving when the timing is right. Conviction is a core strength; satisfaction signals alignment and frustration signals drift. Kim counters that Deep State viewers will study this report for what makes her tick, and that they strike hardest when she is most aligned -- the No Joy Program.

Karen describes the white, open areas of Kim's design as places where outside influence is received and amplified. When her motivation of hope slips into guilt, the shadows follow: overcommitting and the urge to prove herself, emotional overwhelm from absorbing others' feelings, scattered focus, and pressure to act before the correct moment. Her design is logical rather than emotional, and her body knows before her mind does. Kim relates this to her practice of never making major decisions in anger, stepping away when needed, and using music, the gym, and walks with her dog to return to herself.

Kim's Life Work theme is service through correction and perfecting. Operating with a personal focus, her path involves facing her own challenges, researching, and gaining new perspectives to transform herself and then guide others. Rather than carrying the load alone, she fulfills her purpose through bonding, collaboration, and co-creation. Karen highlights Kim's gift of critical awareness, which pinpoints the source of a weakness, challenges what is out of alignment, and weighs multiple opinions to find what is truly valuable. That drive requires stillness to receive insights, and it serves to clear whatever stands in the way of joyous vitality and pure Source Energy. Kim ties it to Harmony with the Divine Plan.

Karen describes her public role as Pioneer and Messenger: learning through trial and error, identifying what is not working, and communicating practical solutions. Eight of her 26 life aspects (31%) fall in communication and action expression, and others project the rescuer role onto her, creating expectations she is not designed to fulfill. Her ideal environment is Passive Mountains -- "rare air" offering distance, calm, and a 360-degree view of power structures without pressure to act. Kim describes building her own "mountain" amid chaos with headphones, credits her move from Durango, and tells of reading operatives who offer friendship. Her digestion is Hot Thirst, meaning warm food and drink. Karen closes on hope as connection to Source and guilt as disconnection. Part Two continues with the annual cycle itself.
