Summary

On the 25th anniversary of 9/11, Kim declassifies what happened behind the Twin Towers and Pentagon attacks. The operation was contracted a year in advance through the same worldwide operative structure that gathered around the Harvest Moon meetings. George "the Viking," whose company Spectrum operated from K Street in Washington, D.C., received advance orders for the event. Bush Jr. took direction from Dick Cheney, while Bush Sr. remained highly placed within the Order of the Black Sun; above them, orders for an operation of this scale came through the Machine.

The Twin Towers were rigged and imploded. The planes departed and landed safely in Pennsylvania, while passengers were taken into underground facilities. Osama bin Laden was paid to serve as the fall guy, and military, intelligence, White House, and K Street operatives carried out their assigned parts. Trucks around the Towers moved people, equipment, explosives, and chemicals rather than gold. Radiation and disease-causing agents contributed to the deaths and illnesses that followed, while virtual reality rooms within the Simulation provided additional control over the event. The Pentagon attack helped generate the fear and patriotic response needed to launch the "war on terror."

Kim closes with her memory of watching 9/11 unfold while on maternity leave with her three-week-old child, followed by a firsthand account from her friend Dawn. Dawn was in Manhattan for meetings near the World Trade Center when the PR firm she was scheduled to visit received advance warning not to enter Manhattan, not to open its offices, and to alert its employees. She recounts the panic in the city, fighter jets overhead, her evacuation north by train, and learning the following day that an entire community of people had been warned in advance.
