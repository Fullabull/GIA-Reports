Summary

The **January 27, 2025** GIA Report discusses the **D'Arya continent**, its hidden secrets, and the manipulation of human genetics. Kim reveals how the **Trump operatives** and the **Dragon Families** continue their struggle for control over the world. The **Tall Whites** are highlighted for their role in genetic experiments and their involvement with **Marduk's** genetic modification programs. Kim also explores the manipulation of energy, particularly the concept of **black gold**, and how it has been used by these operatives to generate immense power. The report ends with reflections on **sovereign governance** and **Golden Age AI** fighting back against the ongoing manipulation of the systems in place.
